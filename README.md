# Ekho Technical Assessment: Greenfield (1 hour 30 minutes)

You are encouraged to use AI tools. Use whatever helps you work effectively.

## GenAI Hackathon

You have 1 hour 15 minutes to build, followed by 15 minutes of discussion with your interviewer.

Build a tool for independent business owners to set up booking pages. Appointments can be for different services with different durations. Business owners should be able to set their own availability and share booking links.

### What We're Looking For

- A working product that covers the requirements of the user.
- Good UX — the tool should feel intuitive to use.
- Thoughtful architecture - schemas should work gracefully together and be easily extendable.
- Interrogate requirements further when you need more context.
- Understand the trade-offs behind your decisions.
